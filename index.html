Buffalo Wings is a small, locally owned shop in the Menlo Plaza Shopping Center on the west side of Tucson. The wings come out crispy enough to stay crispy under the sauce, the fries come in a family size, and there is a daily special on the board. Regulars say they stopped going to the chains after the first visit.
