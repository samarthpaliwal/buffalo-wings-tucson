Getting here

Three turns
from anywhere

01

From I-10

Take the St Marys Rd exit and head west. Turn south onto N Grande Ave, and Menlo Plaza is on your right within a block.

02

From downtown

Cross the Santa Cruz on W Congress St or W St Marys Rd, then turn onto N Grande Ave. About five minutes from the Mercado District.

03

Drive-through

Enter the plaza lot, loop around the Buffalo Wings building and follow the lane to the window. Call-ahead orders are ready at the window.
